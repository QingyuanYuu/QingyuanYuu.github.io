## Overview
A reinforcement learning project training a Super Mario agent with Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) on `gym_super_mario_bros`. The agent learns from pixel observations to navigate, avoid enemies, and complete levels.

## Highlights
- Algorithm: PPO (stable policy gradients, clipped objective)
- Env: `gym_super_mario_bros` with custom observation/action wrappers
- Results: Successfully clears World 1-1 and 1-2

## Tech
- Python, PyTorch
- OpenAI Gym interface, `gym_super_mario_bros`
