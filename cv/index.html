Research & Experience

  • 2026.06 - Present
    Co-First Author
    ROAM: Memory-Driven Active Observation for Open-Vocabulary Object Retrieval
    Advisor: Prof. Xiaolong Wang, UC San Diego
    • Designed the perception, memory, exploration, and navigation subsystems; proposed a memory-completeness-driven revisit scheduler that plans multi-degree-of-freedom viewpoints and improved object recall from 28.8% to 46.7%.
    • Built YOLO-World, SAM2, and 3D association pipelines, and introduced anchor-keyframe-relative pose storage to eliminate accumulated SLAM drift, keeping median object-localization error within 12 cm.
    • Evaluated the system on GOAT-Bench, improving memory-retrieval success from 0.166 to 0.238 and reducing navigation path length to known targets from 55.1 m to 5.8 m, with a 7.2× improvement in SPL.
    • Deployed the system on a Dexmate Vega wheeled humanoid robot and designed an adaptive recovery strategy that reduced exploration runs invalidated by getting stuck from approximately 33% to 0%.
  • 2025.11 - 2026.04
    Co-First Author
    HuG: Humanoid Whole-Body Affordance-Aware Grasping with Active Perception
    Advisor: Prof. Xiaolong Wang, UC San Diego · CoRL 2026
    • Proposed the Perceive-Align-Grasp (PAG) embodied-intelligence framework and designed a Bayesian-belief active perception algorithm in the Perceive stage; maintained a probability distribution over a yaw–height viewpoint grid and improved target detection success under severe occlusion from 57.5% to 92.5%.
    • Bridged perception and downstream whole-body control by aligning active-perception viewpoints online with the input distribution of a CVAE-based grasping policy, and used multidimensional point-cloud quality metrics to determine when to stop observing; stabilized post-detection grasping success at 87.0%.
    • Integrated Python, PyTorch, ROS2, OpenCV, and point-cloud processing modules; deployed and validated the complete system on a Unitree G1 humanoid robot equipped with a 3-DoF active head and dexterous hands, improving end-to-end success on multi-shelf tasks from 50% to 87.5%.
    • Trained exclusively on simulation data without real demonstrations and achieved zero-shot sim-to-real transfer, reaching 90% (seen) and 85% (novel) whole-body grasping success rates on the real robot.
