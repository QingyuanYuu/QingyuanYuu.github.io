I am a Mathematics-Computer Science undergraduate at UC San Diego (expected March 2027), working on embodied intelligence with Prof. Xiaolong Wang. My research focuses on active perception, world models, and coupling perception with whole-body control for humanoid manipulation and navigation. I am a co-first author of HuG (CoRL 2026) and am currently developing ROAM, a memory-driven system for open-vocabulary object retrieval.
